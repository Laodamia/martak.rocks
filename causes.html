Causes Worth Your Support

by Marta Krzeminska

This list can serve as inspiration for non-material gifts for me, or an inspiration on where to direct your money if you want to support a good cause, a talented person, a value-aligned organisation.

Note: Supporting important causes and/or giving to charity is proven to make you feel happier not just temporarily, but also in the long run.

💙 Effective Altruism

👉 Take the Giving Pledge. Commit to donating 10% of your income to effective charities.

You can make a one-off donation, or commit to a regular donation.

  • Giving What We Can (GWWC). You can split your money between Global Health, Animal Welfare and Existential Risk. The research team together with evaluators and grantmakers will allocate the total fund money to maximise impact. This is where I donate monthly.
  • GiveWell (GW). A meta charity that evaluates the effectiveness of other charities. You can set up a recurring donation, or donate once. You can choose to donate to GW and leave it to their discretion which of their recommended charities they support, or choose one of their charities yourself.
  • Animal Charity Evaluators (ACE). A meta charity evaluating effectiveness of charities in the area of animal welfare. There is often donation matching around December. (Matching means your donation will be doubled using money from other sources/philanthropists)

🍪 Other Projects

Below is a list of creators, services, and organisations:

  • which have helped me learn & grow as a person,
  • whose free services we often take for granted,
  • whose creators promote principles I stand by,
  • whose work inspires me,
  • which help improve our understanding of the world.

🤖 AI

  • AI Explained (YouTuber) — Patreon Support. The most up-to-date and thorough summaries of AI research news and industry developments, created without any agenda other than that of understanding what's going on in this field.
  • Robert Miles (YouTuber) — Patreon Support. Easy-to-understand explanations of research concepts in AI.

📚 Education

  • Khan Academy — I used it a LOT to review stats & probability when I thought I'd become a data scientist.
  • Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia)
  • Gapminder — One of the most reliable data sources and creators of data tools to help understand global economy and global progress.
  • Andy Matuschak & Michael Nielsen (Patreon support) — Creators of interactive essays on complex topics as a way to explore and work on the idea of new tools for thought.

🥳 Rationality

  • Cosmic Skeptic (YouTuber) — Patreon Support
  • Sam Harris (Podcast & Meditation App) — one of the most influential thinkers I follow. Even if you yourself don't need subscriber content, your support will help spread the message of rationality. They offer content and the app for free to those who can't afford it.

✨ Inspiration

  • Exurb1a (YouTuber) — inspirational YT channel. Gives a new perspective on life and helps me create distance to myself and my "oh-so-important" problems. Sometimes even fall into a meditative state.
  • The Pursuit of Wonder (YouTuber) — Inspirational YT channel. Creative videos to bend your mind on itself, read in a hypnotising voice.

Marketing Consulting

by Marta Krzeminska

Below is a table listing marketing competencies that Marta Krzeminska can offer as a marketing consultant.

This list is very likely non-exhaustive and doesn’t cover adjacent skills in areas such as operations, data analysis, data reporting/stories, customer experience, or outreach/relationship building and community building.

  • Notes on features
    • Example tasks are for illustrative purposes, the lists are not comprehensive.
    • Proficiency levels are estimated and self-reported. They are relative to the skills where Marta has the highest level of proficiency.
    • When the proficiency is low and enjoyment high, it means that Marta suspects she’d enjoy figuring it out.
